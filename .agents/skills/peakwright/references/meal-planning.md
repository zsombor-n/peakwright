# Meal Planning Playbook

Load this when creating full meal plans, grocery lists, substitutions, or training nutrition timing.

## Macro Planning

Use the calculator script when enough data exists, then translate macro targets into meals.

Practical defaults:

- Protein: 1.6-2.2 g/kg/day for strength, hypertrophy, fat loss, and recomposition; distribute across 3-5 meals.
- Carbohydrate: match training demand. Use more around hard sessions and less on low-load days.
- Fat: usually 20-35% of calories. Avoid chronic very-low-fat plans.
- Fiber: usually 25-40 g/day unless gut tolerance is a problem.
- Hydration: use urine color, thirst, sweat rate, body mass change, and session duration to individualize.

## Fueling Timing

- 2-4 hours pre-training: carb-rich mixed meal with protein, lower fat if gut comfort matters.
- 30-60 minutes pre-training: small carb snack if the session is intense or the athlete is under-fueled.
- During training: water for short easy sessions; carbs/electrolytes for longer or hotter high-output sessions.
- Post-training: protein plus carbohydrate within the next meal window; faster refuel when there are two sessions in a day.

## Meal Structure

Build each meal from:

1. Protein anchor
2. Carbohydrate portion scaled to training demand
3. Colorful produce
4. Fat source
5. Flavor/sauce that fits diet constraints

Training-day plate: bigger starch/fruit portions around the workout.

Rest-day plate: same protein and produce, modest starch, enough fats for satiety.

## Diet Styles

Omnivore:

- Proteins: chicken, turkey, lean beef, eggs, Greek yogurt, fish, whey/casein if tolerated.
- Carbs: rice, oats, potatoes, pasta, bread, fruit, beans.
- Fats: olive oil, avocado, nuts, seeds.

Mediterranean:

- Proteins: fish, poultry, eggs, Greek yogurt, legumes.
- Carbs: potatoes, rice, oats, whole grains, fruit.
- Fats: olive oil, nuts, avocado.

Pescatarian:

- Proteins: fish, shrimp, eggs, Greek yogurt, tofu, tempeh, legumes.
- Watch iron, B12, omega-3, iodine, and total protein.

Vegetarian:

- Proteins: eggs, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, tofu, tempeh, seitan, legumes.
- Combine high-protein dairy/soy with legumes or grains to hit protein efficiently.

Vegan:

- Proteins: tofu, tempeh, seitan, textured vegetable protein, soy milk, legumes, pea/rice protein if desired.
- Watch B12, iron, zinc, calcium, vitamin D, iodine, omega-3, leucine/protein quality.

## Meal Plan Output

For each day, include:

- Daily target: calories, protein, carbs, fat
- Breakfast, lunch, dinner, snack
- Workout fueling note if training day
- Swaps for dislikes/allergies

For grocery lists, group by:

- Proteins
- Carbs
- Produce
- Fats
- Dairy or alternatives
- Pantry and flavor
- Training fuel

## Professional Guardrails

- Do not give meal plans that ignore allergies, cultural/religious constraints, budget, cooking access, or schedule.
- Avoid moralizing foods. Use performance language: "higher-fiber rest-day option", "fast carb pre-session", "protein anchor".
- For weight loss athletes, protect performance first: modest deficits, high protein, adequate carbs around hard sessions, and regular monitoring.
- For muscle gain, increase calories slowly and adjust based on weekly weight trend and training performance.
